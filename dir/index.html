About IT Staffing Services (ITSAC) Contract

IT Staffing Services contracts provide temporary IT staffing augmentation services on a time (hourly) basis. Contracted staff render services and are compensated on an hourly basis.

More information on how to order IT staffing services through the DIR IT Staffing program can be found on the DIR IT Staffing program website.

For additional information about the DIR Cooperative Contracts program, please visit the DIR Cooperative Contracts program website.

IDEA Technologies LLC (ITL) is pleased to be an approved vendor authorized to provide IT staffing services to State and Local government agencies through the Texas Department of Information Resources (DIR) IT Staffing Services Contract (ITSAC) # DIR-CPO-5540

This contract vehicle allows Texas state agencies, public institutions of higher education, public school districts, and other eligible local government entities to obtain IT staffing services through the DIR Cooperative Contracts program.

Through this contract, eligible entities may work directly with IDEA Technologies LLC (ITL) to procure IT staff augmentation services in accordance with DIR program guidelines.
